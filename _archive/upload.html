🔑 SHA-256 Hashing

SHA-256 (Secure Hash Algorithm 256-bit) generates a unique 64-character hexadecimal fingerprint from your file content.


✔ Even a 1-byte change produces a completely different hash.

✔ The hash is one-way — it cannot be reversed to get the original file.

✔ Any tampering will change the hash and break the blockchain chain.


SHA-256 is generated locally in your browser using the Web Crypto API.
