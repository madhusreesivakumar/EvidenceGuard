Client-Side Cryptographic Protection

Private keys are generated locally in your browser using the Web Crypto API (ECDSA P-256) and encrypted with your passphrase using PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations) and AES-256-GCM before saving to local IndexedDB. The EvidenceGuard server never receives your private key or passphrase.
⚠️ Important: If you lose your passphrase or clear your browser data, your key cannot be recovered. Download an encrypted backup immediately.
